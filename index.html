Regulatory Disclosures & Risk Factors

ARN-145058 | EUIN-E028717 (AMFI Registered Mutual Fund Distributor)

Statutory Risk Factors – Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully. Mutual Fund Schemes do not assure or guarantee any returns. Past performances of any Mutual Fund Scheme may or may not be sustained in future. There is no guarantee that the investment objective of any suggested scheme shall be achieved. All existing and prospective investors are advised to check and evaluate the Exit loads and other cost structure (TER) applicable at the time of making the investment before finalizing on any investment decision for Mutual Funds schemes.

Commission & Intermediary Disclosure – Ameva acts solely in the capacity of an AMFI Registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN-145058). We deal in Regular Plans only for Mutual Fund Schemes and earn trailing commissions from Asset Management Companies (AMCs). Details of commission structure are disclosed to clients at the time of investment or upon request. Option of Direct Plan for every Mutual Fund Scheme is available to investors offering advantage of lower expense ratio without distributor intermediation. We are not entitled to earn any commission on Direct plans, hence we deal exclusively in Regular plans.
