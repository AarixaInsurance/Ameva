In accordance with SEBI Master Circular guidelines on mutual fund distribution, Ameva Mutual Fund Distributors (ARN-145058) discloses the commission structures receivable from Asset Management Companies (AMCs) across different scheme categories for Regular Plans.

Direct Plan vs Regular Plan Notice: Ameva distributes Regular Plans of mutual funds and receives trail commission from AMCs. Every mutual fund scheme is also available in a Direct Plan with a lower Total Expense Ratio (TER) without distributor intermediation. We do not earn commissions on Direct Plans.
