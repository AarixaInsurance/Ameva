Part A – Customer–MFD Distribution Agreement (19 Clauses)

STATUTORY TERMS
1. Purpose & Scope of the Agreement
This Agreement sets out the terms and conditions governing the mutual fund distribution services provided by AMEVA (ARN-145058, EUIN-E028717) to the Investor under applicable SEBI regulations and AMFI Code of Conduct.
2. Nature of Relationship (Distributor vs Advisor)
AMEVA acts strictly as an AMFI-Registered Mutual Fund Distributor and NOT as an Investment Adviser (RIA). No investment advice or portfolio management service is provided.
3. Due Diligence & Know Your Client (KYC)
The Investor agrees to complete KYC verification through a SEBI-registered KRA and submit all required documentation including PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, and bank details.
4. Investor Risk Profiling
Risk profiling is conducted to assess the Investor's financial capacity, risk tolerance, and horizon. Results are based on investor self-declaration and are indicative only.
5. Investment Objectives & Categorization
AMEVA assists the Investor in identifying scheme categories aligned with declared financial goals and liquidity needs. The final scheme selection is made solely by the Investor.
6. Product Appropriateness & Due Diligence
AMEVA shall perform appropriateness checks before facilitating any mutual fund transaction to confirm that the scheme category matches the investor risk profile.
7. Risk Disclosures (Market, Credit, Liquidity)
Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks. Units are not bank deposits and values fluctuate with underlying market valuations, interest rate cycles, and macroeconomic factors.
8. Regular vs Direct Plan Disclosure
AMEVA distributes Regular Plans only and receives trailing commission from AMCs. Direct Plans (with lower expense ratios and without distributor commission) are available directly with AMCs.
9. Distributor Remuneration & Commission Structure
AMEVA earns permissible trail commission from AMCs as per SEBI regulations. No upfront commission is charged or collected from investors. Commission schedules are publicly available.
10. Transaction Processing & Cut-off Timings
All subscriptions, redemptions, and switches are processed through official RTA/AMC platforms or exchange routing channels subject to applicable SEBI cut-off timings.
11. No Guarantee of Returns
AMEVA does not assure, promise, or guarantee any return, capital protection, dividend payout (IDCW), or NAV growth. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
12. Investor Responsibilities
The Investor is responsible for providing accurate information, updating contact/bank details, reviewing transaction confirmations, and making the final investment decision.
13. Confidentiality & Data Protection (DPDP Act 2023)
AMEVA maintains strict confidentiality of investor data and processes information solely for servicing, transaction fulfillment, and statutory compliance under the DPDP Act 2023.
14. Conflicts of Interest
AMEVA adheres to AMFI Code of Conduct and discloses any material conflict of interest that may arise in the course of mutual fund distribution.
15. Limitation of Liability
AMEVA shall not be liable for losses resulting from market movements, AMC/RTA processing delays, bank settlement failures, or incorrect investor details.
16. Grievance Redressal Mechanism
Grievance Redressal Officer: Rajesh Kumar Gupta | Mobile: +91 98100 47256 | Email: rajeshgupta@ameva.in, amevamfd@gmail.com
Investors may also escalate unresolved grievances to the concerned AMC, SEBI SCORES, or SMART ODR portal.
17. Record Keeping & Audit Trail
AMEVA maintains investor onboarding records, risk profiles, and transaction logs for the minimum statutory period mandated by SEBI/AMFI regulations.
18. Termination of Relationship
Either party may terminate the distribution servicing arrangement by written notice. Outstanding mutual fund investments remain safely held with the respective AMCs.
19. Governing Law & Jurisdiction
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts in Gurugram, Haryana shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising hereunder.
