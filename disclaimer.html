Our Role

Ameva Mutual Fund Distributors is an AMFI Registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN-145058, EUIN-E028717). We act only as a distributor of mutual fund schemes. We are not a SEBI Registered Investment Adviser, Portfolio Manager or Research Analyst.

Any information or discussion regarding schemes is incidental to distribution and is not investment advice. We do not hold discretionary authority over your investments. The final investment decision is always yours.

Important Payment Notice: We do not accept cash or funds into our own account under any circumstances. All investment payments are made directly to the respective mutual fund AMC / authorized clearing house.

Regular Plans and Our Remuneration

We distribute only the Regular Plans of mutual fund schemes and receive commission (trail commission) from Asset Management Companies. Every scheme also offers a Direct Plan, which has a lower expense ratio because no distributor commission is paid. You may invest in the Direct Plan directly with the AMC without our involvement.

Details of commission payable to us are available in the Commission Disclosures section of our website and upon request.
